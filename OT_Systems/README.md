# OT Systems — Comprehensive OT Security Evaluation

## Executive Summary

This project delivers an in-depth Operational Technology (OT) security assessment for a large Irish automotive enterprise ("Combulator"), which operates 400 Siemens PLCs (S7-300, S7-400, and S7-1500 series) across 10 national branches and serves 50,000 customers globally.

The evaluation identifies and analyses the primary threat actors targeting the automotive sector — including APT groups (Scattered Spider, Lapsus$), ransomware-as-a-service operators (Qilin), and supply chain attackers — and maps their known tactics to the organisation's specific attack surface. The report examines IT-OT convergence risks, supply chain vulnerabilities (which account for 67% of automotive cyber incidents), and the financial and operational impact of successful attacks, drawing on real-world incidents against JLR and Nissan subsidiaries.

Findings are translated into prioritised security recommendations tailored to both legacy and modern Siemens controller environments, balancing operational uptime requirements with the need for robust cyber resilience in an industry-facing environment.

**Key skills demonstrated:** OT/ICS security, threat intelligence analysis, attack vector mapping, supply chain risk assessment, IT-OT convergence risk, Siemens PLC environments, sector-specific threat actor profiling, and security advisory writing.
